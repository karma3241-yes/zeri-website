About

We started by building for home.

Zeri is a software company in Thimphu. We help businesses turn the way they work into software that holds up.

The software was rarely the hard part.

Zeri began with one job: make it easier to get a ride or send a parcel around Thimphu. That one job turned into two phone apps, an admin console and the system that connects them.

Building it taught us something. Code can be fixed. The hard part was the work around it. Who approves what. Which steps get skipped when everyone's busy. What should happen when a phone loses signal halfway through a trip.

Every business has its own version of those questions. So now we take on software jobs for other people too, from POS and hotel systems to whatever yours needs.
