Privacy

Your details, in plain words.

This page covers this website only. Last updated 7 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into our contact forms: your WhatsApp number and your message, and your name and business name if you give them.

Why we collect it

To reply to you and talk about your project. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't sell it.

Who can see it

The Zeri team, and FormSubmit, the service that delivers the form to our inbox. We won't share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it to reply to you and carry out any work we agree. After that, we delete it.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies, and it has no analytics or advertising trackers. It does load its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google can see your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you. Send your request through our contact page.

The Zeri ride app

The rider and driver apps have their own privacy policy.
