Ventures

We build our own things too.

A venture is a product we start ourselves and have to live with. The first one is the Zeri ride app.

Zeri

Rides and deliveries across Thimphu, from one app.

Simple on the outside. Four systems underneath.

Getting a ride or sending a parcel across town sounds simple. Then you try to build it.

You need a fare people can see before they book. A driver who gets the right offer at the right moment. A map that understands how people here give directions, by landmark. And someone at a desk who can check an application or a payout without calling three people.

We built all of it ourselves, and it's the reason we trust our own advice.

Rider app

Book a ride now or schedule one, see the fare first and watch your driver arrive. Send a parcel from the same place. Women riders can ask for a woman driver first.

Driver app

Apply to drive, take ride offers, navigate to pickup and follow what you've earned.

Admin console

The web side of things: fare zones, driver applications, shipments, payout checks and reports.

Backend

The part nobody sees. It handles dispatch, routing, wallets and notifications for both apps.

Built with React Native, MapLibre, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis and Firebase.

Meet Achu.

Software is easier to love with a face on it. Achu is the red panda in the blue cap who stars in the Zeri reels and app listings. A small reminder that a product needs people to care about it, not just a working login screen.
